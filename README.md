# 🚀 Vahan Bazar – The Future of Bike Buying, Selling & Riding  

Vahan Bazar is an **AI-powered two-wheeler marketplace web app** that simplifies the way users **buy, sell, and experience bikes, scooters, and EVs**.  
From browsing verified listings to booking test rides, and from AI-based price predictions to personalized recommendations, **Vahan Bazar is the one-stop digital hub for all two-wheeler needs.**

---

## 📌 Problem Statement  

The current two-wheeler ecosystem is **fragmented and inefficient**:  
- Buyers struggle with scattered information, lack of price transparency, and no digital support for test rides.  
- Sellers often face unfair resale valuations and limited reach.  
- Dealers spend heavily on inventory management with poor customer engagement.  

---

## 💡 Our Solution – Vahan Bazar  

Vahan Bazar bridges this gap by providing a **unified, intelligent, and future-ready platform** that:  
- Enables users to **browse, compare, and book test rides** digitally.  
- Helps sellers with **AI-driven resale price prediction** and quick listings.  
- Supports dealers through a **dashboard for inventory, leads, and promotions**.  
- Enhances decision-making with **smart EMI & fuel cost calculators, EV suitability checks, and AI recommendations**.  

👉 In short: **A complete two-wheeler journey, simplified through technology.**

---

## 🔑 Key Features  

- ✅ **Advanced Listings** with images, specs, pricing  
- 🔍 **Smart Search & Filters** (brand, fuel type, price, mileage)  
- ⚖️ **Side-by-side Comparisons** of models  
- 💰 **EMI & Fuel Cost Calculators**  
- 🚲 **Used Bike Selling** with AI price prediction  
- 🏬 **Showroom Directory & Test Ride Booking**  
- 🚀 **Upcoming Launches Hub**  
- 🤖 **AI/ML Innovations**:  
  - Personalized Recommendations  
  - Multilingual Chatbot Assistant  
  - EV Suitability Analysis  
  - Dynamic Price Alerts
