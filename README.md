
## 📌 Problem Statement  

The current two-wheeler ecosystem is **fragmented and inefficient**:  
- Buyers struggle with scattered information, lack of price transparency, and no digital support for test rides.  
- Sellers often face unfair resale valuations and limited reach.  
- Dealers spend heavily on inventory management with poor customer engagement.  

---

## 💡 Our Solution – Vahan Bazar  

Vahan Bazar bridges this gap by providing a **unified, intelligent, and future-ready platform** that:  
- Enables users to **browse, compare, and book test rides** digitally.  
- Helps sellers with **AI-driven resale price prediction** and quick listings.  
- Supports dealers through a **dashboard for inventory, leads, and promotions**.
